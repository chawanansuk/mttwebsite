How to work out how many boxes to order →

How they're packed

Sizes 000–1 arrive as sealed lion-brand sachets, ready to sell as-is. Sizes 2–7 come as bunches on a tag — easy to pull one at a time, and easy to hang on display.

Trade units, decoded
  • 1 dozen = 12 pieces
  • 1 gross = 12 dozen = 144 pieces — sizes 6 and 7 come 144 to a box, exactly one gross.
  • Size 000 is also written 3/0, and 00 as 2/0 — some makers label them that way.

Wholesale FAQ

How many in a gross?
A gross is 12 dozen — 144 pieces. Sizes 6 and 7 come exactly 144 to a box, so one box is one gross.
Minimum order? Can I mix sizes?
No minimum — sachets or bunches are fine. Wholesale rates start at box quantity, sizes can be mixed in one order, and more boxes means a better rate.
Counted or weighed?
Every box is counted, not weighed and estimated — so shops and factories can plan materials exactly, with no padding.
Tax invoice available?
Full tax invoices are available — send your company name, tax ID and address on LINE when ordering.
Shipping upcountry?
Nationwide. Cost depends on weight and destination — tell us the province and how many boxes and we'll quote first. Big orders go cheaper by ground freight.
Can I visit the Sampheng shop?
Yes — we're in Sampheng, Bangkok. Message on LINE first to check the sizes you want are in stock so the trip isn't wasted.
