FAQ

How long is size 6?
About 7.5 cm (75 mm / 3"), measured cap to coil; makers vary by ±2–3 mm.
How many per box?
About 144 pins per box (counted, not weighed) — also sold by the dozen bunch or 144-pin gross.
Is size 6 enough for tarps?
Yes for typical tarps — for very heavy or high-tension work, step up to size 7 (85 mm).
