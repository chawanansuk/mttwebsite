What Lion brand is

Lion brand is the safety-pin brand of M.T.T. Hardware, a tools and hardware shop in Sampheng operated by Wynns Tools Hardware (Thailand) Co., Ltd. Every size is nickel-plated spring steel in silver, with the point hidden in a cap. Sizes 000 to 1 come in sealed Lion-brand packets; sizes 2 to 7 are strung in bunches of 12 and boxed.

FAQ

Who makes Lion-brand safety pins?
Lion brand is the safety-pin brand of M.T.T. Hardware in Sampheng, operated by Wynns Tools Hardware (Thailand) Co., Ltd. This site is the brand's source.
Can I buy Lion-brand pins from other shops?
Yes. Several retailers resell them in shops and online. The product is the same; price and service differ by shop. Ordering from us directly gets you counted boxes, tax invoices, and the people who know the product best.
How many sizes are there?
Ten sizes, from 000 at 19 mm to 7 at 85 mm, all nickel-plated spring steel in silver.
How do I choose a size?
Match the job: sizes 000–00 for price tags, 0 or 2 for race bibs, 4 for diapers, 5–7 for canvas. See the size guide.
