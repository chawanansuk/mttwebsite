000 or 00 — which one

Just 4 mm apart — 19 versus 23 mm — but it matters when tagging. Size 000 nearly disappears on the product, ideal for pendant and earring cards where the pin shouldn't compete with the piece. The catch: staff must pinch it with fingertips, which gets tiring and drops a lot over a full shift. Size 00 is barely longer but noticeably easier to hold, which is why shops tagging hundreds of pieces a day pick it. When in doubt, start with 00.

Pins vs tagging guns and string

A tagging gun is far faster in bulk, but leaves a permanent hole and is unusable on fine knits or silk. String and plastic seals look good and harm nothing, but threading them one at a time is slow. Pins sit in between: reasonably quick, removable without a trace in the small sizes, and reusable — which is why shops reach for them on delicate fabrics and on items customers try on and put back.

000 (1.9 cm) for the smallest tags like jewellery, 00 (2.3 cm) for general garment tags — when unsure, start with 00.
