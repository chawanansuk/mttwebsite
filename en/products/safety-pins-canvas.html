FAQ

Which size for tarps?
Size 6 (75 mm, 1.25 mm wire) for ordinary tarps; step up to 7 (85 mm, 1.35 mm) for thick tent fabric or high tension; size 5 (65 mm) is enough for drapes and stage work.
Size 6 vs size 7?
Two things: 75 vs 85 mm long, and 1.25 vs 1.35 mm wire. Size 7 holds more and passes through more layers, but leaves a bigger hole — so take the smallest that still works.
Will they rust outdoors?
Nickel-plated spring steel, not true stainless. Good for events and temporary covers; months of weather will start to show. For permanent fixing, use grommets or rope.
Per box? Minimum order?
Size 5 is 216 a box; 6 and 7 are 144 (one gross), packed as tagged bunches. No minimum — bunches are fine, wholesale starts at box quantity.
