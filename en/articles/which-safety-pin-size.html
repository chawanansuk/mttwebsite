Which safety-pin size for which job? The 000–7 guide

By M.T.T. Hardware, Sampheng · Published 21 Aug 2026 · Updated 4 Oct 2026

Thai-market safety pins come in 10 sizes, from tiny 000 (1.9 cm) to jumbo 7 (8.5 cm) — bigger number, longer pin. Pick by the job first, then the size. This table settles it.

Rule of thumb — smallest pin that holds for tags (000–00), mid sizes for garments (0–2), 3–4 for thick fabric, 5–7 for canvas and outdoor work.

Measuring a pin correctly

1

Close the pin first

Clip the point into its guard. Maker sizes are the closed length, not the open one.

2

Lay it on a ruler

Lay the pin flat with the guard end on zero. Keep it straight, because a tilted pin reads long.

3

Read at the end of the coil

Read the overall length from the guard to the far end of the coil, not just the shaft and not the inner edge of the coil.

4

Match it to the size table

Compare with 19, 23, 28, 32, 38, 46, 56, 65, 75 and 85 mm, which are sizes 000, 00, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7. Makers differ by about 2–3 mm, so take the nearest size.

Common questions

What are sizes 2/0 and 3/0?

2/0 is size 00 and 3/0 is size 000. They are different names for the same sizes.

How many pins in a bunch or a gross?

A bunch is one dozen, 12 pins, and a gross is 144. Per-box counts for each size are on the wholesale page.

Which size pins race bibs?

Size 2 (3.8 cm) is the most common choice, and some organizers use the smaller size 0 (2.8 cm). Four pins per runner.

How do I match a pin I already use?

Photograph your pin against a ruler and send it on LINE and we'll match the size, since makers can differ by ±2–3 mm.
