Lion-brand safety pins: buying direct vs from a reseller

By M.T.T. Hardware, Sampheng · Published 9 Oct 2026

Lion-brand safety pins are sold in many places: by us, the brand owner, and by shops that resell them. This guide covers how buying from the source differs from buying from a reseller, when a nearby shop is the better choice, and how to compare offers fairly.

It's the same product

Lion brand is the safety-pin brand of M.T.T. Hardware, and several shops resell it in stores and online. If the packet or box carries the Lion-brand name, it's the same product you get from us. What differs is the price, the units sold, how the count is done, and each shop's service. See the Lion-brand page.

What you get from the source

1

All ten sizes in one order

Sizes 000 to 7 in one place, mixed in a single order, so you don't have to hunt for a size another shop lacks. See the size table.

2

Every box is counted

Boxes are counted, not weighed and estimated. The per-box count for each size is in how many pins per box, so jobs that need exact numbers, like races, can be planned precisely.

3

Buy any amount, no minimum

Buy by the packet, 12-pin bunch, 144-pin gross or box, with no minimum. Wholesale pricing starts at a full box.

4

Quotations and tax invoices

We issue quotations and full tax invoices to companies, agencies and schools. Send the details on LINE when you order.

5

Large orders in one go

A recent race organiser's order was 100 boxes of size 0. Work out your own box count on the race pins page.

6

Talk to people who know every size

Send a photo of the job on LINE and our team will help you pick the size before you order in bulk.

When buying from a nearby shop makes sense

Shops that resell Lion-brand pins serve plenty of buyers well. If you're near one and need a few packets today, want other things from that shop in the same order, or already have an account there, buying from them makes sense. The product is the same.

For jobs that need several sizes, exact counts or paperwork for reimbursement, ordering from the source usually gets it done in one order.

How to tell it's Lion brand

Look for the Lion-brand name on the packet or box. Sizes 000 to 1 come in sealed Lion-brand packets; sizes 2 to 7 are strung in bunches of 12 and boxed. If you're unsure, send a photo of the packet or box on LINE and our team will take a look.

Common questions

Can other shops sell Lion-brand pins?

Yes. Several retailers resell them in shops and online. The product is the same; price and service differ by shop.

What does buying from the source cost?

It depends on the size, unit and quantity. Send the size and quantity on LINE for a quote, then compare it as a price per pin using the table above.

How do I become a reseller?

Message us on LINE with the sizes and quantities you want, and we'll quote wholesale box prices. You can mix sizes in one order.

Why compare price per pin, not per box?

Because box counts differ by size: size 2 has 864 pins per box while size 6 has 144. Similar box prices can mean very different prices per pin.
