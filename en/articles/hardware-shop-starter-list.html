What a new hardware shop needs: a starter tool list from the catalogue

By M.T.T. Hardware, Sampheng · Published 9 Oct 2026

Shops opening a hardware section often aren't sure what to stock first. This guide lists eight basic tool groups from the WYNNTOOLS catalogue and our own M.T.T. brand, with example codes, pack and carton sizes, and how to place a first order without buying a carton of everything.

Eight basic tool groups

1

Wrenches

8- and 10-inch adjustable wrenches, a combination wrench set and a hex key set cover most nut-and-bolt work. See adjustable wrench sizes and the full wrench catalogue.

2

Pliers

Combination, long-nose and diagonal cutting pliers plus locking pliers turn up in almost every job. See which pliers for which job.

3

Screwdrivers

Magnetic impact screwdrivers in several lengths, plus interchangeable-bit sets for customers who want one handle for many tips. See the screwdriver catalogue. Read how screwdriver tips differ.

4

Hammers and chisels

A claw hammer for nailing, a rubber mallet for work that mustn't be marked, and wood chisels. See the striking tools catalogue.

5

Electrical

Wire strippers, crimpers and a digital multimeter for electricians and home repairs. See the electrical catalogue.

6

Cutting

Utility knives with spare blades, hacksaw frames and blades, and files. Blades are consumables customers come back for. See the cutters, files and saws catalogue.

7

Levels

A 2-foot spirit level for building work and a mini level for small installs. See the measuring tools catalogue. Read how to use a level and plumb bob.

8

Padlocks

M.T.T. silver-lion padlocks in 30 and 40 mm, and keyed-alike sets that all open on one key for shops, warehouses and rental blocks. See the padlock catalogue. Read how to choose a padlock.

Placing a first order without overstocking

1

Start with packs, not a carton of everything

On a first order you don't yet know what your customers buy most. A pack each of many items gives a fuller range than a carton of a few. Ask for pack and carton prices on LINE.

2

Keep the codes for reorders

Catalogue codes stay the same, so when something sells out just send the code and quantity on LINE.

3

Put small items near the counter

Cutter blades, M.T.T. jet lighters and Lion-brand safety pins are small items for the counter, and you can order them with your tools.

4

Ask for a tax invoice when you order

We issue full tax invoices. Send your company name, tax ID and address with the order.
